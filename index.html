Who am I?

I'm Md Hassan, an M.Tech student at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur. I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science from MANUU, Hyderabad with a CGPA of 8.96/10, where I developed a strong foundation in computer science, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven systems.

During my undergraduate studies, I worked on projects spanning AI, machine learning, cybersecurity, networking, and intelligent systems, including encrypted VPN traffic classification, intrusion detection, phishing detection, and scalable software solutions. I'm GATE-qualified in Computer Science (IIT Roorkee 2025) and Data Science & AI (IIT Guwahati 2026), UGC NET 2025 qualified, a Founding Member of E-Cell MANUU, and currently working as an ML Engineering Intern at FlyRank AI.
